# Prediction markets

Binary event contracts are records of their own, priced in the lake, their
outcomes recorded as each venue determines them, and grouped by the event
they belong to.

- **Their records and groups:** [Event groups](event-groups.md).
- **Their prices and outcomes:** [The lake](the-lake.md).
- **A venue's plugin:** [Add a prediction-market plugin](../how-to/add-a-prediction-market-plugin.md).
- **Plugins:** [Integrations, filtered to event contracts](../how-to/integrations.md?asset=event-contract).
