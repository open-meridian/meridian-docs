# Crypto

Crypto assets are records by their CAIP-19 identifier, priced in pairs
against a quote asset on each venue that lists them.

- **Their records:** [Instruments](instruments.md) and
  [One ID per instrument](one-id.md).
- **Their prices:** pairs' closes, bars, trades and quotes in
  [The lake](the-lake.md), and
  [Read trades and quotes](../how-to/read-trades-and-quotes.md).
- **Holding and trading them:** [The book of record](the-book-of-record.md)
  and [The order lifecycle](the-order-lifecycle.md).
- **Plugins:** [Integrations, filtered to crypto](../how-to/integrations.md?asset=crypto).
