# Equities

Shares, ETFs and funds are held by unit, named by their FIGI where the
platform's master knows it, and priced in the lake by their daily closes,
bars, trades and quotes.

- **Their records:** [Instruments](instruments.md) and
  [One ID per instrument](one-id.md); a company and its declared dividends in
  [Companies and corporate actions](companies-and-corporate-actions.md).
- **Their prices:** [The lake](the-lake.md), and
  [Read trades and quotes](../how-to/read-trades-and-quotes.md).
- **Holding them:** a custody plugin's statements, the custodian's activity
  and the book: [The book of record](the-book-of-record.md) and
  [Using a custody plugin](../how-to/using-a-custody-plugin.md).
- **Trading them:** [The order lifecycle](the-order-lifecycle.md) and
  [Route orders through a connectivity plugin](../how-to/route-orders-through-a-connectivity-plugin.md).
- **Their options:** [Options](listed-options-and-futures.md).
- **Plugins:** [Integrations, filtered to equities](../how-to/integrations.md?asset=equity).
